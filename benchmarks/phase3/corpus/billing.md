# Billing and plans

Kestrel Cloud bills monthly in arrears. Usage is metered per second and rounded up to the next full cent on each line item.

## Plans

The Free plan costs 0 dollars and includes 5 GB of storage and one small instance. The Pro plan costs 49 dollars per month per organization and includes 500 GB of storage and priority support. The Enterprise plan costs 499 dollars per month and adds single sign-on, a dedicated account manager and a 99.99 percent uptime commitment.

## Invoices and payment

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and are payable within 14 days by card or bank transfer. Add your tax identification number in Billing settings to have VAT handled correctly on every invoice.

## Refunds

You can request a refund within 30 days of a charge, provided the charge was a plan fee and not metered usage. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Changing or cancelling a plan

Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated for the rest of the month. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. Data is kept for 30 days after cancellation and is then deleted permanently.
